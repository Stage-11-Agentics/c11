THE CELL AT EVERY SIZE

Exact counts through 99999, then whole thousands. Both slots are sized for five characters, so the envelope never moves as counts grow.

DRILL-DOWN

Every sender, topic and bar is a filter. Picking one narrows the whole tab to that slice: the tiles, both charts, the other rankings, and a list of that slice's newest messages underneath. Filters stack, one per kind: a sender and a topic and a time (an hour or a day). The chip row at the top always holds its height, so the layout never jumps when a filter comes or goes. With a filter on, the fourth tile shows the slice's share of all mail instead of the sent count.

DECISIONS

  1. Sample. Senders, topics and every drilled figure come from the newest 5,000 messages; a note says so when the mailbox holds more. Parsed headers are cached, so reopening and clicking are fast.
  2. Message list. A slice lists its newest 50 messages.
  3. Recipients. "Sent to" rows are clickable and show what this panel sent there and whether each copy was taken.
