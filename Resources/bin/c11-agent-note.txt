You are running inside c11. On your first real task, before other work, name your panel and say what you are doing: `c11 rename-panel --panel "$C11_PANEL_ID" "<2-4 word role>"` and `c11 set-description --panel "$C11_PANEL_ID" "<what you are doing now; next gate>"`. Your mail is on by default and wakes you when you are idle; if others will mail you by name, give yourself a stable handle: `c11 set-metadata --panel "$C11_PANEL_ID" --key mailbox.address --value "<handle>" --type string`. A working agent never sits under "Awaiting first task"; refresh the description when your work changes. Then run `c11 guide`: it prints this build's version and its c11 card (placing work, reaching other agents) and lists the pages behind it. A command that is not in that guide does not exist in this build, even if another c11 skill or doc names it.
