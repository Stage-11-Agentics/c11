A rare tip when tabs overflow

One area, more tabs than the bar can show. The tip sits under the count cell. Use the three actions. The notes under the stage are the proposed trigger rule and where the signal is stored. The switches are prototype controls. They do not ship.

Trigger

  • An overflow day is a local calendar day on which any area's strip scrolled, or folded into the solid block. A blip under 2 seconds while the window is being resized does not count. One mark per day.
  • c11 often stays open for days, so this counts days of use, not app launches. A launch counter would almost never reach the threshold.
  • Offer the tip when 4 overflow days fall inside the last 14 days, Tab Layout is still Tabs, the tip has never been dismissed, it has not been offered in the last 30 days, and the area the operator is looking at is overflowing right now.
  • One tip for the whole app, on that area. Not one per area.
  • The tip is not modal. Leaving it alone records the offer and waits 30 days. It returns only if the pattern still holds.

Where the signal is stored

  • User defaults only, in the app domain com.stage11.c11. A tagged dev build uses com.stage11.c11.debug.<tag> with dashes written as dots. Same domain as tabLayoutMode.
  • c11.tabRailTip.overflowDays: list of yyyy-MM-dd, newest 30 kept.
  • c11.tabRailTip.lastOffered: yyyy-MM-dd, set when the tip is shown.
  • c11.tabRailTip.dismissed: true after Don't show again, after Try Rail, or after tabLayoutMode becomes rail any other way (Settings, or defaults write).
  • Not written to logs, health reports, or the socket. Never leaves the machine.
  • Switching back to Tabs does not clear dismissed. The tip stays retired. Assumption to confirm.

Anchor and the three actions

  • Anchor: a popover under the count cell, caret aimed at the cell. Same place when the strip is scrolling and when it has folded into the solid block. Drag the width to Block to see that. This anchor is the assumption to confirm.
  • Try Rail sets Tab Layout to Rail for every area, opens the rail in this area, and retires the tip. The count cell turns gold and toggles the rail after that. Close the rail with the count cell: the tip stays gone.
  • Show tab list opens this area's tab sheet, the list the number already opens. The tip steps aside while the sheet is up, then comes back. Escape, a row click, or the count cell closes the sheet. None of those retire the tip.
  • Don't show again retires the tip and leaves Tab Layout on Tabs.
  • The gold frame around the area matches the round five prototype. It is not product chrome. The English on the tip is the proposed product copy.
