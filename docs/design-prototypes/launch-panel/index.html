Panel, not popover — centered on its pane. No entrance animation, no transient dismissal — it appears the frame you right-click, centered over the pane whose A button you clicked, and stays until you close it (esc, ✕, A). Launching sets the system default and closes the panel — no checkbox, no pin gesture: whatever you launched last here is what a plain A-click launches next, and the ● + DEFAULT tag simply show where that landed. Persistence covers everything short of launch: browsing, dropdown tweaking, focus loss. Left = User Configurations: full saved recipes, including custom system prompts (gold chip). Right = Harnesses: one row per enabled harness, agent + effort dropdowns preset to that harness's defaults — tweak either and ↵ for a quick one-off launch — a changed dropdown goes gold and a quiet + save button materializes on the row (no toast; saving is right there or nowhere). The circled ⚙ in the header is the panel's one route to management (Settings — Agents & Launch, ⌘,). Perf note for the impl: the v1 slowness is the SwiftUI .contextMenu trigger + NSPopover animates=true; v2 intercepts right-mouse-down directly and shows a borderless child panel with animation off.

One mental model, two views. The panel's two columns are exactly these two settings objects: User Configurations (ordered recipes with custom system prompts; one carries the ● default) and Harnesses (machine-detected; toggle = appears in panel; base recipe = the panel dropdowns' defaults and what configurations inherit from). Editing here is inline — no modal-in-modal; the panel reflects changes live. The panel's single footer row (⌘,) deep-links here, keeping the panel itself focused on launching.
