c11 tab bar QoL

Three fixes, one PR. Everything below is live: click the collapsed headers, drag tabs and rows between panes. The selector at the top of section 3 changes the drop indicator in every mock on the page. Pick one option per section.

1 · The collapsed header: from a floating chip to a solid primitive

Today (left) the header is a rounded chip floating in the bar, and the list opens as a pop-out with an arrow. Both options below fill the bar edge to edge with square corners, and the list opens as a solid sheet flush under the bar, like a drawer pulled out of it.

Current TODAY

Chip inset inside the bar, arrow popover. The popover is its own window, so a drag that starts in it dies when it dismisses.

A · Solid block RECOMMENDED

The active tab fills the bar as one solid block. The count sits in its own square cell, and it turns gold while the sheet is open. The sheet has square corners, a gold top rule, surface numbers in a column, and a gold left rule on the active row. Rows drag out of the sheet: it stays open while you reorder inside it and gets out of the way once the cursor leaves.

B · Active tab + stubs

The active tab keeps its normal tab look, so the mode change doesn't jar. Every other tab shrinks to a square stub showing its surface number, with an activity underline. You can click a stub or drag it straight to another pane without opening anything. Once the stubs run out of room, "+N ▾" opens the same solid sheet.

Drop target: another pane (drag sheet rows or stubs here)

2 · Dragging out of the list

You can try it in the mocks above: drag any row out of an open sheet into the target pane. The behavior we build:

  1. A press-drag on a row starts a tab drag; a click still selects. The row is one drag source, not a Button with a drag bolted on.
  2. The sheet stays open while the drag is inside it (reorder, with a gold rule between rows). It hides once the cursor leaves, so the panes beneath are exposed as drop targets. It never tears down the drag source mid-drag.
  3. A drop anywhere a normal tab can land works: another pane's tab strip, a split edge, the neutral zone.

3 · Drop indicator: show exactly where the tab will land

Today it's a 2pt line. A drop in the neutral zone (the empty stretch between the last tab and the buttons) lands at the end, but the line draws next to the last tab, far from your cursor. Pick one:

Frozen: hovering the neutral zone

Ghost slot: a tab-sized slot opens at the landing index with the dragged tab's title in it, and the whole neutral zone tints gold, so the drop reads from anywhere in it. Bar: a 4pt gold bar with end caps, full height, plus the same zone tint.

Frozen: hovering between two tabs

Mid-strip, the ghost slot pushes the neighbors apart, which previews the result instead of pointing at a seam.
