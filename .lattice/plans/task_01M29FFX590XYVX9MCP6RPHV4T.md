# C11-215: Serialize c11 builds: one xcodebuild per machine, enforced by a lock

Operator ruling 2026-09-11 (during the 0.65.1 run, load average hit 250 with two delegator xcodebuilds plus a c11-unit build-for-testing sharing the box; swift-frontend starved at 4% CPU for 35 minutes): never more than ONE c11 build on a machine. Mechanism, not just a rule: scripts/with-build-lock.sh takes a machine-wide lock (/tmp/c11-build.lock, mkdir-atomic, owner pid + tag recorded, stale-owner detection, waiting message every 30s, C11_BUILD_LOCK_TIMEOUT), and every local build entry point (reload.sh, reloads.sh, reloadp.sh, test-unit-local.sh, test-unit.sh) runs xcodebuild through it. Raw xcodebuild in prompts and skills is replaced by the wrapper. CLAUDE.md gets the HARD RULE; the lattice-orchestrator Standard Clauses gain 'one build at a time, always via the wrapper'; installed skills synced. Fast behavioral test: two wrapped commands never overlap.
