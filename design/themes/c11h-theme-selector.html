Prototype notes

DECIDED WITH ATIN

  1. One theme, day and night. No night theme, no macOS Light/Dark link, no status line. (18:59)
  2. The picker offers the seven c11h themes, then Your themes from the themes folder. Phosphor, Blueprint, Command Deck, Obsidian, Radical and Spatial leave the app. (15:30)
  3. Picking a theme also switches the terminal colours when the theme has a matching scheme. Stage 11 and c11h have none and leave the terminal alone. (15:30)
  4. Appearance is the whole design: Interface Size first, then the theme swatches, bigger, in a page that scrolls. "App Chrome UI Scale" becomes Interface Size with a plain description; Custom keeps its slider. (20:31)

HOW IT BEHAVES

  • A pick repaints the window at once and is kept across restarts.
  • Each swatch is the top-left corner of your real window, drawn in that theme with your real workspaces, and redraws when they change while Settings is open.
  • c11h themes set <name> sets the same one theme; themes get prints it.
  • Today you have Stage 11 by day and Nocturne by night. The upgrade keeps Stage 11, the one you picked last, as the theme.

YOUR FOLDER TODAY

  • Blueprint, Command Deck, Obsidian and Spatial are copies of the retired themes, so they show under Your themes until you move them out.
  • c11h never touches that folder. One line moves them beside the earlier backups: themes.before-h6/
